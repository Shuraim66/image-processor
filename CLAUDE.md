## Rules (must stay in effect)
- `SESSION_RECOVERY.md` is historical context; do not edit it.
- `box-is-source-of-truth.md`: copy facts (contents, counts, features, age, name) come from the printed box, not partial photos.
- `fal-spend-approval.md`: never call fal.ai without the user's explicit approval of an exact figure. Paid slots only: CatalogHero, Lifestyle, FeatureProduct. Real cost: $0.0515/image (`FAL_MODELS` in `fal_listing.py`). `MAX_REFS=3`. Spend log in `_fal_usage.json` / `processed_products.csv`.
- `catalog-image-approach.md`: real photo + local template text — **CatalogHero is photography only.** `CATALOG_HERO_V2` in `fal_listing.py` (replacing the old CatalogHero formula on 2026-09-22 — see Completed this session) allows a richer styled photo (environment, props, composition, close-up detail insets) and may show real packaging in frame, but must **never add any AI-drawn text** — no headline, no feature callouts, no slogans, nothing beyond what's physically printed on the real product/packaging as photographed. Headline and feature-callout text stays with the free local `FeatureCard`/`Hero_titled` templates, which pull verified facts from `product.json` — deliberately kept separate so AI-generated text can never misspell something or invent a claim the box doesn't support. CatalogHero also must never move a packaging logo into a header/corner/watermark position, and must never treat packaging illustration art as photographic evidence of what's physically included (real product photos outrank packaging photos outrank packaging artwork). Other slots (Lifestyle, FeatureProduct, Angle, Open, Box, Detail) are unchanged.
- `stock-and-backlog.md`: list only `units > 2`; `stock = units - 1`; rest to `backlog-products/`.
- `shopify-store-conventions.md`: 31 fixed Types; prefixed tags (`age:`, `occasion:`, `feature:`, `play:`, `sub:`); handle = folder lowercased, no ages; SKU is short code (e.g. `TGS-AQUAFLOW-SWIM-CAP`).
- Photo placement: revised images are extra angles, never replacements (`_archive/superseded_photos`).
