## Cross-check before publishing
- Compare every generated image against its source photo. Generative renders redraw
  the product: printed text becomes approximate lettering, clasps and seams shift.
  `/Detail` crops are where this shows first.
- Do not publish a spec that isn't in the **Verified** lines above without confirming
  it against the physical product or your supplier's sheet.
- Main listing image: no watermark, no added text, product on pure white.
